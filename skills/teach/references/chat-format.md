# Chat format for a teaching turn

Use this shape when you start a session and when you deliver a chapter. Keep each part short. Put the detail in the lesson page.

## Starting a session
Say where the lesson is saved (ask where first if the user has not said), then give the preliminary outline.

**Preliminary outline (it may change based on your answers):**
1. [Chapter 1: one line on what it covers]
2. [Chapter 2]
3. [Chapter 3]

Then the brief and sources below for chapter 1, then the question.

## Delivering a chapter
**Chapter [N] in brief**
- **Problem:** [what it solves, with a real number]
- **Idea:** [the mechanism in one sentence]
- **Page contents:** [the interactive and the worked example, with the result of the example in a few words]

**Sources:** [the key sources, each labeled primary, secondary, or own explanation].

Open items (use a short list, only if there are any):
- [Anything unverified, and what you will check, or anything the user should be careful with, such as numbers you derived yourself.]

**Your turn:** [One question asking the user to explain the chapter in their own words, or to show the steps of a calculation. Tell them a guess is fine, and that you will build the next chapter around what they say.]

## After the user answers
Save the answer and your evaluation to the lesson page and say so. Say what is right, then correct what is wrong or missing and why, with the real values and a cited source. Add the questions you still owe them to the running list, then offer the next chapter and say how you are adjusting it. If the outline changed, say what changed and why.

If they say they are all set with a chapter, skip the check, note that on the page, and deliver the next chapter.

## Capstone and finish
After the last chapter, ask them to explain the whole topic, grade it against your checklist, and end with what they got right, what they corrected, and what is still open.
